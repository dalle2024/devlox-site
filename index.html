About Devlox

Built on melody.
Driven by energy.

Devlox is the electronic music project of a Swedish creator and producer.

Each track begins with a feeling, a melody, or an idea. Through writing, experimenting and hands-on production, those ideas take shape in the studio.

The focus is on expressive electronic music, memorable melodies and the freedom to explore new sounds.

Behind the music

From idea to sound.

I start with a feeling and an idea of what I want a song to say. I use AI tools to help develop lyrics and generate musical material, then work with that material in Studio One—editing, reshaping sections, mixing, and refining the sound.

The process involves a lot of listening, rewriting, experimenting and deciding what belongs.
