About Devlox

Electronic energy.
A country heart.

Devlox is the music project of Swedish creator and producer Daniel.

Each song begins with an idea, a feeling, or something he wants to put into words. Through writing, reworking, and hands-on production, those starting points take shape in his home studio.

His upcoming EP, Beyond the Edge, brings together four songs exploring connection and the uncertainty of moving forward.

The four-track EP

Four songs.
One chapter.

From the warmth of Southern Sun to the closing title track, Beyond the Edge follows a journey through closeness, vulnerability, and change.

Right Here With Me finds belonging in another person. If I Disappear brings uncertainty into the picture, before Beyond the Edge opens the door to what comes next.

Behind the Music

The story behind the sound.

I start with a feeling and an idea of what I want a song to say. I use AI tools to help develop lyrics and generate musical material, then work with that material in Studio One—editing, reshaping sections, mixing, and refining the sound.

The process involves a lot of listening, rewriting, and deciding what belongs. Beyond the Edge brings together four songs I chose to carry forward.
